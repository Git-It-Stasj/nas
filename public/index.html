About

Engineer first. Teacher always.

I'm Nastasja Terry, and I've spent my career joining things that don't naturally belong together and making the bond outlast the pressure. For more than seventeen years I've worked in aerospace — quality assurance, FAA certification, aftermarket engineering, and training technical teams on the 787 and A350 across more than ten countries. Then I watched AI get handed to people with no framework for how to use it, and I decided to build one. Today I lead The Architect, where I advise leaders and speak on practical, responsible AI adoption, and I founded SheForge, an AI app suite I engineered for women entrepreneurs. I teach the way I was trained: strategy first, then tools you can put to work on Monday morning.

What your body still needs

Less hunger doesn't mean fewer needs.

Peptide-based medications, such as the GLP-1 class, work in part by lowering appetite, so you eat less. That can help the scale. It also shrinks your chances to get protein, fluids, fiber, vitamins and minerals. Four things deserve a plan.

01

Protein

When weight comes off, some of it can be lean mass, not only fat. Protein at each small meal, plus strength training, is the usual way to protect muscle. Small meals leave little room, so protein goes first.

02

Fluids

A good share of daily fluid comes from food. Eat less and you may drink less without noticing. Steady sips through the day beat catching up in the evening.

03

Fiber and gut comfort

Digestive changes such as constipation or nausea are commonly reported. Gentle fiber, fluids and regular small meals are common starting points. Symptoms that persist belong with your provider.

04

Vitamins and minerals

Fewer meals means fewer chances to cover the basics. Ask your provider or a registered dietitian whether any gaps need filling, and check before adding any supplement.

Questions people ask

Straight answers, in plain language.

How much protein should I eat per day when I'm trying to lose weight?

Needs depend on your body size, age and activity, and guidance is usually given per kilogram of body weight. A registered dietitian or your doctor can set a target that fits you.

What can I eat when I'm not hungry?

Many people do better with small, frequent, protein-rich foods and drinks, and some find cold options easier. Begin 30's plant protein can be stirred into small meals.† If a medication is lowering your appetite, ask your healthcare provider about your nutrition.

Why am I not losing weight in a calorie deficit?

Many things can play a part, including how portions and calories are tracked, sleep, activity and stress. No supplement replaces those basics. If the scale has stalled for weeks, talk with your healthcare provider.

How do I stop snacking at night?

Habits that start earlier in the day tend to help: protein at dinner, a set time when the kitchen closes, and enough sleep. The Wellbeing+ app lets you track those habits day by day.†

Can I use Begin 30 products if I take a weight-management medication?

Talk with your healthcare provider before starting any supplement, especially if you take a prescription medication.

Why does weight loss feel harder after 40?

Changes in hormones, sleep and muscle mass can all play a part. Strength training, enough protein and good sleep are common places to start, and your healthcare provider can advise you on the rest.
